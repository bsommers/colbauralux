# How to play

[Back to README](../README.md)

## Goal
Capture every Cheese Wheel on the map, or wipe out all rival ships and wheels. You lose when you own nothing.

## Rules
- Owned wheels spawn kittens continuously. Bigger and higher-level wheels spawn faster.
- A wheel's garrison grows as it produces. At 24 units a wheel levels up (more production, higher cap 30 / 60 / 100); at 54 it levels up again. Garrison is not consumed by leveling.
- Ships of opposing teams annihilate 1:1 on contact.
- A hostile ship arriving at a wheel removes one garrison unit; at 0 the wheel flips to the attacker.
- Sending takes a percentage of garrison (25 / 50 / 75 / 100 percent, keys `1` to `4`). Ships stream out gradually, not all at once.

## Controls
Drag from one of your wheels, swipe over other wheels of yours to add them, and release on a target wheel to send. Release on empty space to cancel. Tap works too: tap yours, then tap the destination. Double-tap empty space or press `A` to select all. See the [README](../README.md#controls) for every key.

## Factions
| Faction | Colour | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Colby Jack Clowder (you) | orange | kittens |
| Rat Pack | purple | rats |
| Mouse Mob | cyan | mice |
| Commander Cheddar | red | the boss |
| Unclaimed Cheese | yellow | neutral, free to take |

AI personalities: rusher (all-in waves), turtle (attacks only when clearly ahead), expander, balanced, sniper (tight margins, avoids big fleets). Difficulty: Kitten, Normal, Hard, Nine Lives.

## Campaign
15 levels from First Nibble to The Big Cheese, with stars for beating the par time. Skirmish mode generates a fair random map from a seed (`?seed=N`).

## Tips
- Grab nearby neutral wheels first; production compounds.
- Send 100 percent only when you are sure; a half-empty wheel is easy to flip.
- Let rival factions fight each other in three-way levels, then strike.
- Big wheels with high level are worth more than many tiny ones.
